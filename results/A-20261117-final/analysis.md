# Repeated-run analysis

Area and delay are shown as one pair only when stable across all measured runs. Failed or incomplete strategies are excluded from the normal Pareto comparisons.

The A/D/runtime frontier using median runtime is descriptive. Overlapping observed runtime ranges are marked as an unresolved runtime difference; separated ranges are also not a statistical significance claim. No official score is inferred.

The area sum is a descriptive statistic only. Per-case medians are not independent extra cases, and per-case winners are not combined into a fictitious solver.
